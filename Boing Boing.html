This 8×8 display uses vacuum and silicone instead of LEDs

Mark Frauenfelder 12:11 pm Tue Aug 25, 2026
Air-powered pneumatic pixel display panel. Credit: soiboi/YouTube

A YouTuber who goes by soiboi soft built an 8×8 pixel display that uses pneumatics and stretchy silicone instead of LEDs. The effect is mesmerizing and appealing.

The orange rubber panel covers 64 cavities. When a vacuum forms in one of the cavities, it produces a dimple in the rubber. Soiboi explains how even the transistors are pneumatic. He demonstrates the display by playing Snake on it.

More about air-powered tech:

Non-electronic displays and pneumatic engineering have both shown up on Boing Boing before: a 3D-printable version of the 1965 Think-a-Dot toy, which flips an 8-pixel, 2-bit display by dropping marbles into three holes, and a silicone gripping hand that inflates through a length of plastic tubing, no 3D printing or power supply required. So have Fraunhofer's eight-legged exploration spiders, whose elastic bellows bend and extend their limbs on pneumatic pressure alone.
