Popular Warehouse stage at Leeds Festival forced to close for the second time this weekend

The Warehouse stage at Leeds Festival has been forced to close for the second time this weekend.

Ahead of his set at 1:30pm, Sheffield DJ Silva Bumpa took to social media to let fans know that the show would no longer be happening due to overcrowding.

He wrote: “So so sorry Leeds Fest but due to over crowding my set was cancelled last min I was just behind the stage and got told right before such a massive shame.”

This is the second time that The Warehouse stage has had to close at Leeds Festival this weekend, as yesterday, Julian Fijma had to shut his set down due to too many people being outside.

He said: “Leeds, little update. Unfortunately, we had to shut the stage down.

“There was way too many people outside and yeah, we couldn’t keep going. But I’ll be back soon.”

The Warehouse stage also had to close at the event’s sister festival in Reading on Friday afternoon, as the DJ duo Prospa, who were supposed to be performing, had to apologise to fans as their set was also called off. 

The duo said that this has happened at festivals around the UK, and that they think it’s time for the capacity of dance tents to be doubled.

They said: “We have seen this happen before at larger festivals in the UK.

“It’s time to double the capacity of tents or put dance music on the main stage.”
