The King's Head in Little Marlow fails hygiene inspection

30th August
By James Richings News and Sports Reporter

A popular country pub has been handed a low food hygiene rating following its latest inspection.

The King's Head, on Church Road in Little Marlow, was awarded a food hygiene rating of one, meaning "major improvement necessary", after an inspection by Buckinghamshire Council on July 21.

The rating, which was last updated on August 28, highlighted areas where inspectors said improvements were needed.

Inspectors judged the pub's food hygiene and safety standards to be "generally satisfactory".

However, the venue was told improvements were necessary in relation to structural compliance, which covers the condition and maintenance of the premises.

The most serious concerns related to confidence in management, which was rated as requiring "major improvement necessary".

Despite the latest rating, The King's Head remains popular with customers, holding an average score of 4.4 out of five from 434 Google reviews.

The King's Head can request a re-rating inspection once any required improvements have been made.

Food hygiene ratings explained

  • 5 - Very Good: Hygiene standards are very good and fully comply with legal requirements.
  • 4 - Good: Good hygiene standards with only minor improvements needed.
  • 3 - Generally Satisfactory: Meets legal requirements but there is some room for improvement.
  • 2 - Improvement Necessary: Some issues were identified and improvements are required.
  • 1 - Major Improvement Necessary: Significant improvements are needed to meet required standards.
  • 0 - Urgent Improvement Necessary: Urgent action is required to improve hygiene standards.
