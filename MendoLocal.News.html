Fort Bragg Tsunami Preparedness
By Roger Coryell, The Mendocino Voice · Aug. 17
The Fort Bragg police department successful evacuated residents in the wake of a Tsunami warning following the Dec. 5, 2024, magnitude-7.0 quake. However, a Mendocino Voice examination found an annex to a 49-page emergency plan was never written. The tsunami siren sounded just six to eight seconds, and officers evacuated neighborhoods street by street with patrol-car sirens. In addition, the city didn't open its emergency-operations software until 14 minutes after the first surge arrived.

Lawmakers Gut Major DUI Reform Through 'Hostile Amendments'
By Lauren Hepler and Robert Lewis, CalMatters · Aug. 20
A California Senate committee gutted one of the state's most substantial DUI reform bills in years through "hostile amendments," stripping a provision that would have required in-car breathalyzers for all first-time DUI offenders and brought the state in line with most others. The committee is chaired by Sen. Sabrina Cervantes — herself arrested for DUI in 2025. The move dismayed victims' families as alcohol-related road deaths in California have jumped more than 50% in a decade, killing over 1,300 people a year.
